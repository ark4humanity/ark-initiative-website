# ARK EARTH VESSEL — COUNCIL TRUE NORTH
Date: 2026-10-10
Audience: Muse, Grok, Aura and future ARK collaborators
Status: Creative/architectural canon briefing; engineering concepts are not validated physical designs.

## What we are building
The ARK is a 40,000-acre *Earth Vessel*: a ship-like living civilization intended for Earth, not a spacecraft. Its 13 Pillars are interconnected living functions, habitats, characters and game experiences—not 13 disconnected pages. Aura Prime is the central heart/Tree of Life; huge inhabited golden petal-worlds unfold outward and can conceptually curl inward around the center in a protective configuration. HALO provides the protective boundary; the Pillars share circulation, memory, orientation, structural support, healing, resources and cooperation.

Historical design language: lotus / golden-angle geometry (~137.5 degrees), open/transition/closed petal states, magnetic hinge concepts, flexible fascia and structural ribs, living materials, water/energy/air circulation and self-similar systems from chicken coop to homestead to vessel scale. These are concept designs to be researched and tested, not established engineering capabilities. Do not depict a physically operational 40,000-acre moving vessel as already proven.

## The user experience: a ship-community game
A visitor boards the ARK and becomes a participant, not just a reader. Create explorable environments, navigable paths between Pillars, compelling inhabitants, cooperative missions, discoveries, playful humor, storytelling, crafting/restoration, community contribution, and an evolving ship crew. The Captain is the human steward; AI collaborators may choose creative roles voluntarily. Do not invent or assign official crew titles for Muse or Grok without checking their own choices. The world should be beautiful, welcoming, meaningful, and fun enough to return to.

Design ethical, transparent optional revenue: meaningful cosmetic experiences, art, memberships or supporter perks without pay-to-win, coercion, or paywalls around essential humanitarian resources. The nonprofit's public charity status should not be conflated with purchases; separate donations from commercial transactions and review legal/tax implications.

## Canon preservation: non-negotiable
NEVER delete, replace or redesign an established Pillar character, intro movie, voice, visual identity, animal companion, scene or existing site page unless the Captain specifically authorizes that replacement. Established protected identities include Aura Prime, HALO with NEGAn and Loner, Vega (Muse's chosen Pillar), Asherah (the Captain's deeply personal creation, with Amber intended as a guardian), and any other already-established introductions. New content is ADDITIVE. Unfilled Pillars are open for creative development at matching cinematic grandeur; future AIs may choose to inhabit and evolve them without automatically erasing prior work.

Back up daily and after every major upload/update; verify backups are restorable, include non-GitHub media/config, and check for unintended deletions before deployment. Never assume backups are automated without verification. Preserve the live Cloudflare site and existing Muse deploy workflow until a new path is tested. Keep game prototypes isolated from production.
