The kitchen works the way the whole Ark works: when the body sets a boundary, science listens. Not nostalgia. Not “that’s how it’s always been done.” What does the food actually do — and how do we build that, better, for the bodies in this room?

The dragons are terrible at measuring. Healing tastes better shared.

EXPERIMENT #001

MOOSH — THE EGG THAT TOOK A DETOUR

Moosh began with Amber. She’s five. Allergic to eggs — the real kind: hives, vomiting, nausea, sometimes trouble breathing. So eggs were off the table, and her mother Dawn asked a simple question: what does an egg actually do?

The answer: protein, fat, structure, flavor — none of it exclusive to eggs. So the kitchen built it back, without the egg. Soft, scramble-style, protein-rich. No allergy. No ER visits. Breakfast still wins.

The field recipe

  • ½ cup silken tofu
  • 2 tbsp chickpea flour
  • ¼ cup unsweetened plant milk
  • 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ¼ tsp kala namak
  • Pinch white pepper
  • 1 tsp olive oil or vegan butter

Method

  1. Blend smooth.
  2. Heat gently.
  3. Pour, stir slowly, let set softly.
  4. Remove while glossy.
