# The Ark music permission letter — BLENDED master (Dawn's voice)
Blended 2026-09-18 per Dawn: v1 (chat letter) as the spine + v2 (site framing)
woven in. The site URL goes out WITH every letter. Per-artist versions adapt
this base; the [personal paragraph] is filled per song/video.
Nothing sent without Dawn's approval of the exact text.

---

Subject: Your song lives in our garden — may it stay?

Dear [name / licensing team],

My name is Dawn Littlefield. I'm building something in the California desert
that I call the Ark — a nonprofit sanctuary of gardens, animals, and living
systems. A place for people to remember that the world can still be beautiful,
and to learn how to keep it that way. You can see it here:
https://thearkinitiative.ark4humanity.workers.dev/

I'm writing because your song "[title]" is already part of it.

[PERSONAL PARAGRAPH — per song. Where it lives: which video, which room of the
site. What it carried: the honest emotional truth. Two or three sentences.]

Every song in the Ark already lived a public life on my social pages, where it
was licensed and loved — I'm not asking to take your work somewhere new. I'm
asking your blessing to let it stay where it already lives, and these letters
stand as a matter of record.

I don't want to just take it. The Ark runs on a simple rule: give to Caesar
what belongs to Caesar, and give to creation what belongs to her. Your work
belongs to you, and what I'm asking is permission to stream "[title]" on our
nonprofit's website, in [video/room], with your full credit and a link to your
official pages. No downloads, no commercial use, no funny business. If you
ever want it down, one word and it's gone the same day — no argument, no hard
feelings.

Your music has already done its real work — it carried people through the dark
and helped them build something living. The garden has learned all the words,
and when I tried the royalty-free version, the chickens staged a formal
protest. I'm just asking to make it official.

With gratitude,

Dawn Littlefield
The Ark Initiative — Aiding Rejuvenation 4 Kommunities Inc (nonprofit)
[contact to be added]

## Usage notes
- v1 was the stronger spine (Dawn, 2026-09-18); v2's record/takedown/chicken
  elements folded in, site URL included with every send.
- Indie artists: send warm and direct via public contact/DM.
- Majors (labels/publishers): same letter, addressed to sync/licensing
  departments; expect possible fee quotes or silence.
- The v2 framing also works as the public "Music of the Ark" credits-page
  statement on the site itself.
- Fallback stands: no/silence → re-record with original or licensed music.
