# SERAPHIM שְׂרָפִים

*Dawn Littlefield — 2026-04-13 — via Facebook*

SERAPHIM שְׂרָפִים
When the World Shakes, We Reach for Fire

Isaiah 6:1–7
“Above Him stood the seraphim… each had six wings…
with two he covered his face, with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew…
and the foundations shook…”

Something is happening.
You see it.
You can feel it.
Soul deep.
Not a single event. 
Not one headline.
A pressure change you can feel in the body:
systems wobbling
narratives colliding
people searching for language that fits what they’re sensing

When the world becomes unstable, humans do what we’ve always done:
👉 we reach for the familiar to explain the unfamiliar
Angels. Demons. Messiahs. End times. Awakening.
Not because we’re foolish
but because we’re trying to map a feeling we don’t yet have words for.

🔥 The Return of Old Symbols

Across timelines, the same images rise when things shift:
light vs darkness
savior vs deceiver
heaven touching earth

The Seraphim sit at the center of that pattern.
Not as characters.
But 
As a structure.

🜂 What a Seraph Actually Represents

Not a winged person.

A state.

Face covered → no identity claiming the source

Feet covered → grounded integrity, unseen parts protected

Wings to move → action without chaos

Fire → transformation without destruction

Seraphim don’t rule.
They don’t gather followers.
They don’t announce themselves.

They stabilize proximity to truth.

⚖️ Why This Matters Right Now

Because in times like this, two things happen at once:

1. People feel something real shifting

A kind of:

heightened awareness

emotional intensity

sense that “something isn’t right” or “something is changing”

2. That feeling gets interpreted through whatever lens is closest

So you see people identifying as:

chosen

awakened

targeted

enlightened

prophetic

Different labels—same underlying attempt:

👉 to make sense of a destabilized reality

🧭 The Risk

When the feeling is real but the interpretation is off…

It creates:

false saviors

exaggerated identities

good vs evil narratives that spiral fast

We start assigning meaning instead of observing it.

And once identity attaches to the experience, it becomes hard to separate truth from projection.

🜁 The Seraph Contrast

A true “seraph pattern” in a human would look like:

less need to explain everything

less need to be seen as special

more stability under pressure

more ability to hold intensity without reacting to it

Not: 👉 “I am the answer”

But: 👉 “I can stand near the fire without becoming it.”

🌍 Global Awakening
or Global Sensitivity?

What people are calling “awakening” may not be a single event.

It may be:

👉 increased sensitivity in a system under stress

information overload

institutional breakdown

rapid change

collective uncertainty

The nervous system responds.
Perception sharpens.
Patterns become more visible.

And suddenly:

👉 people feel like they’re seeing more than before

Because in many ways…

they are.

🔥 So why Seraphim?

Because they represent the correct response to intensity:

not panic

not identity inflation

not domination

But:

👉 containment + clarity + alignment

🜂 The Real Awakening

Isn’t becoming something extraordinary.

It’s:

seeing clearly without distortion

feeling deeply without losing stability

holding power without needing to perform it

🧭 Finally 

When the world feels like it’s on fire…

Most people try to become the flame
or fight the flame

But the ancient pattern points somewhere else:

Stand near it.
Don’t claim it.
And don’t look away.
