From War Game Commander to CEO: Lessons to Save Humanity

By Dawn Littlefield, CEO of ARk4 and Commander of Heroes

"I didn’t choose to fight; I had to. I saw evil enslaving those too afraid to act, and I knew I couldn’t stand by and do nothing. Alone at first, I fought the giants—until a few brave souls joined me. Together, we learned that the future we deserve isn’t given to us; it’s built."

For weeks, I watched as big alliances laughed and bullied smaller players, crushing anyone who dared challenge them. I waited for someone—anyone—to stand up to them. But fear paralyzed them. Maybe it was my severe sense of right and wrong or my history of public service—from firefighter to protection officer—that drove me to act. Perhaps it was my #autism, which gave me a clear-cut sense of justice and fairness. Either way, I knew I couldn’t sit idle any longer.

So, I took action. I started drawing fire from the bullies, launching attacks, mocking them on state chat. The big alliances couldn’t believe one tiny player could cause them so much trouble. But as they turned their focus on me, I saw my chance. I didn’t just want to win—I wanted to create a #future where the weak didn’t have to live in fear.

I wasn’t alone for long. A few brave souls joined me, and together, we stood against the forces of #evil. We fought for those too afraid to fight for themselves, and we won. What followed were two beautiful years of peace. Our kingdom grew and evolved—thriving in harmony like #Eden. We built a place of balance and freedom, a refuge for all who sought it.

But just as #history teaches us, Eden is always vulnerable. Outside forces entered our world—forces that didn’t like a female ruler. They didn’t come to learn or grow; they came to destroy. Though we fought valiantly, we were outnumbered, and the kingdom fell. The experience taught me one of the most important lessons in leadership: No matter how peaceful or balanced your #kingdom is, a strong #defense is always necessary to protect the light inside. This truth has been echoed through the ages, from the fall of civilizations to the battles we fight today. Come join us in building a better future. I know you're afraid, but I'm not. Take my hand and let's build something amazing. I believe in you.
