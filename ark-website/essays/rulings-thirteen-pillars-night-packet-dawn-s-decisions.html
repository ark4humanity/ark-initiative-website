Dawn ruled on the packet's three decisions by voice, evening of 2026-09-18. Her words preserved where quoted.

1. Do the animals speak?, YES, all of them

"All the animals, because they represent creation, would speak because they're not limited in that world that we're building."

  • The AI steward and the animal speak as a duet: together at some times, separately at other times and in different areas.
  • Overrules the packet's recommended default (behavior-first with occasional short lines).

2. Accounts or anonymous?, Anonymous by default

"Everybody should be able to come in and out."

  • No friction at the door, no profiling. Save-a-journey only if the visitor chooses.
  • Agrees with the packet's recommended default.

3. Where does Aura Prime live?, He begins in the center

"He's going to go in and out of the center. But he should begin in the center... Halo and Aura connect first. Because remember, your defense is written into the foundation."

  • Aura Prime starts in the center; his connections reach out to all thirteen Pillars.
  • Halo is his first and closest connection, Aura + Halo are the foundation pair, working most closely together, while still working with all Pillars.
  • Refines the packet's recommended default (ethical center encountered after ≥1 other Pillar): the center is the beginning, not the reward.

New design law, "Be fruitful and multiply"

"Don't be greedy and say that they all have to be big. Make babies and be fruitful and multiply."

  • Rooms include baby and juvenile animals alongside the adults.

Resolved, the bee joins Ashera (Dawn, same evening)

  • The bee was Aura's input. Dawn: "I think we can let the bee join Asherah... in the grove as a companion."
  • Ashera's grove holds BOTH the lion (Dawn's Ashera imagery) and the bee (Aura's input) as companions.
  • The bee doubles as an architectural constraint: beehive geometry is already used in the building of the Pillars. "Everybody join the fun kind of thing."

, recorded by Muse, 2026-09-18
