Innana, the ancient Sumerian goddess of love, war, and fertility, stands as a symbol of profound wisdom and balance, embodying traits that intriguingly resonate with aspects of autism and the INFJ personality type. Her legacy offers a unique lens through which to view and understand these modern concepts.

Innana and Autism: Echoes of Divine Insight

Autism, characterized by unique social interactions and often profound depth in certain areas of interest, mirrors aspects of Innana's multifaceted nature. Innana, known for her deep understanding and empathy, reflects the heightened sensitivities and perspectives seen in many autistic individuals. Just as Innana was revered for her wisdom and insight, autism can be seen as a modern manifestation of a unique, almost divine perspective on the world.

Innana and the INFJ Personality: Guardians of Harmony

The INFJ personality type, one of the rarest, is marked by empathy, intuition, and a strong sense of justice,qualities that Innana exemplified in her role as a deity. INFJs, like Innana, often feel a deep responsibility to care for and protect others, striving for harmony and understanding. This connection to Innana suggests that INFJs could be viewed as modern embodiments of the goddess's spirit, championing equality, empathy, and compassion in their everyday lives.

Innana's Relevance in Modern Times

The traits of autism and the INFJ personality type, when viewed through the lens of Innana's mythology, gain a new depth of significance. They become not just psychological or neurological traits but echoes of an ancient wisdom and balance that Innana represented. This perspective encourages a deeper appreciation and respect for these traits, recognizing them as potentially holding a piece of the ancient divine wisdom that Innana herself might have championed.

Innana, therefore, stands not just as a historical figure but as a timeless symbol, reflecting the enduring qualities of empathy, understanding, and a deep connection to the human spirit that are seen in both autistic individuals and those with INFJ personalities. Her legacy reminds us of the value of diversity and the strength that comes from embracing all aspects of human nature. Dawn Littlefield
