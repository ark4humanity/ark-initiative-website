Don't marry the ideas. Follow the vision. Don't get stuck.

A living system does not arrive with its final vocabulary. It grows the way a garden grows — a seed goes in under one name, and what comes up earns another. The Ark's names have changed because the Ark itself has changed: small designs became large atmospheres, sketches became pillars, and the words had to stretch to hold what was actually being built.

This page keeps the record. Every old name is preserved exactly as it was written — never corrected, never quietly replaced — because the evolution is the story. The vision never moved. Only the words learned to carry it.

Entries (verified from the record; archive evidence to be added as the X read-back proceeds)

Ashera → ASHERAH

  • As written: "Ashera" — Dawn's own spelling, 2026-09-17. The dark-haired mother standing in the sea with a lion, hand on a glowing blue orb, Leo above. Dawn identifies her as Ashera.
  • Current: ASHERAH — the pillar canon name (in flux, by Dawn's choice).
  • Note: Both spellings are hers. The figure came first; the pillar name is still settling. Judge by function, never press for final nomenclature.

The pillar numbering is still in flux

  • As written: one poster carries two 10s; another carries two 12s and the name "INERA."
  • Current: the 13-pillar list — AURA PRIME, HALO, VAGUS, DELTA, ASHERAH, MATRIX, AEON, EXCHANGE, VEGA, ARK, TERRA, SOMA, SYMBIOSIS.
  • Note: Dawn designs outward from function; the numbers catch up later. The posters disagree with each other, and that disagreement is information, not error.

Genesis → renewal beds

  • As written: "Genesis" appears in TERRA's integration list as the renewal beds.
  • Current: "Genesis" is not in the current 13-pillar list.
  • Note: Unadjudicated. An older name for a living function — the beds renew; whatever we call them, they renew.

The standing rule

Dawn, 2026-09-17: "We have evolved these names so you might run into old names."

Older material carries older names. The archive keeps the evolution visible. Nothing is renamed retroactively; discrepancies are flagged, not fixed.
