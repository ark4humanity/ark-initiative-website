When Goddesses Ruled

A Discussion of Our Stolen Future

In the Shadow of Sacred Trees

The Lost Matriarchy

In a time long forgotten, before the dominance of the patriarchal deities of monotheistic traditions, the world knew a balance of power where the divine feminine stood as a co-ruler beside the masculine. The goddesses of old, like Inanna and Ishtar, commanded reverence as embodiments of life, fertility, and wisdom, fostering societies that thrived in equilibrium.

The Divine Feminine A Legacy of Harmony

The worship of these powerful female entities wasn't merely spiritual; it was a societal foundation stone. Temples to goddesses were centers of cultural and political life, where priestesses held sway. It was a time when the sacred feminine and masculine energies were in harmony, creating a balanced world order.

The Rise of the Masculine Divine

An Imbalance Begins

As the wheel of time turned, this balance shifted. The ascendance of male gods saw the marginalization of the goddesses, leading to an era where the masculine divine overshadowed its counterpart, tipping the scales and altering the essence of worship and society.

Yahweh and the Trickster God

A Masculine Supremacy Usurper

In this context, the figure of Yahweh in the Hebrew Bible embodies the complex shift from a balanced divine to a predominantly male deity, seen by some as a usurper of the ancient equilibrium. The portrayal of Yahweh as both nurturing and severe, creator and destroyer, reflects the intricate nature of divine justice and challenges the reader to question the deeper meaning of these ancient narratives.

The World Today

A Call for Rebalance

A fight for the future you should have had.

The echoes of this imbalance are seen today, where dogmatic interpretations of religious texts are used to justify destructive end-time prophecies. The world, aflame with strife and suffering, reflects the consequences of a broken divine harmony, prompting a critical reevaluation of our spiritual narratives.

A Return to Equilibrium

As we grapple with the environmental and societal crises of our times, the ancient tales of powerful goddesses serve as a reminder of a more balanced past. Perhaps it is time to seek inspiration from the sacred feminine once more, to restore harmony in our world.
