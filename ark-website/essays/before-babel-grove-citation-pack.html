The sixteen citation rows

C-01 — Inscriptions exist (scorched pole / clay vessel tab)

FieldContent
ClaimLate Iron Age inscriptions from Kuntillet Ajrud and Khirbet el-Qom mention YHWH together with ʾasherah / Asherata (commonly dated late 9th–early 8th c. BCE).
Evidence classATTESTED
SourceSite inscriptions as summarized in Dawn's Mother They Couldn't Weaponize; scholarly literature widely discusses both sites
Visitor caption"Stone and ink remember a pairing: YHWH… and ʾasherah. The letters are real. What they meant is still argued."
NotesAttest the existence of the formulas. Do not auto-promote "YHWH's wife" as settled fact. Pair with C-02.

C-02 — Goddess vs cult object debate (scorched pole)

FieldContent
ClaimScholars disagree whether ʾšrth means the goddess Asherah/Asherata, a wooden cult object/symbol ("his asherah"), or related readings — debate remains open.
Evidence classSERIOUS HYPOTHESIS

Visitor caption"Some hear a Mother beside the Name. Some hear a sacred wooden sign. The grove keeps both questions lit."
NotesHonest debate banner required on A-03. Never flatten to one conclusion for visitor "fact" voice.

C-03 — Biblical grove / asherah purge language (grove burn)

FieldContent
ClaimHebrew Bible texts forbid planting an asherah beside YHWH's altar (Deut 16:21) and narrate Josiah removing, burning, and scattering an Asherah image from the Temple (2 Kgs 23:6–7).
Evidence classATTESTED
SourceBiblical text tradition — Deuteronomy 16:21; 2 Kings 23:6–7 (also women weaving for Asherah, 2 Kgs 23:7)
Visitor caption"The book itself records the cutting and the burning. Memory of the tree survives inside the order that tried to erase it."
NotesATTESTED as text. Historical reconstruction of every purge event = separate hypothesis layer — do not overclaim archaeology from verse alone.

C-11 — Takarkori genomes / Green Sahara lineage (sand urn)

FieldContent
ClaimAncient DNA from two ~7,000-year-old women at Takarkori rock shelter (SW Libya) reveals a deeply divergent ancestral North African lineage from the African Humid Period ("Green Sahara").
Evidence classATTESTED
SourceNature (2025): "Ancient DNA from the Green Sahara reveals ancestral North African lineage" — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08793-7
Visitor caption"Tilt the sand urn: under the dunes, green wetland light. Two women of Takarkori left a genome the desert almost erased — an old North African lineage, finally readable."
NotesCite the Nature URL; no invented page numbers. Science attests lineage + Green Sahara context. Dawn's leadership/matricentric reading = C-12.

C-12 — Matricentric Takarkori society (sand urn)

FieldContent
ClaimDawn reads burial records, artifacts, and rock art as suggesting matricentric/matrilineal authority among Takarkori / Green Sahara peoples.
Evidence classSERIOUS HYPOTHESIS
SourceDawn Littlefield, Matriarchs of the Green Sahara (2025-04-13)
Visitor caption"The urn remembers sovereign women. Whether they ruled as matriarchs is a living question — genetics gave lineage; ceremony still asks who led."
NotesThe Nature paper focuses on ancestry/isolation/pastoral cultural diffusion — not a confirmation of matricentric governance. Keep banner.
