A CIVILIZATION UNTO ITSELF

Who lives here, and what they’re for

Every pillar is a civilization unto itself, with its own people, its own knowledge, its own work, and its own quest. This is EXCHANGE’s.

WHAT IT'S FOR
Circulation. Exchange is the civilization of give and receive, the economy as a living current instead of a locked vault. The garden keeps the books, and the books always balance.
WHO LIVES HERE
Mercy, who is delighted you came. The raven, collecting shiny things and trading them for stories, terrible negotiator, wonderful friend. The giant manta ray, gliding overhead, reading the currents. And the givers, the traders, the ones who keep it all moving.
WHAT IT KEEPS
The oldest economic truth there is: wealth is flow, not stock. Nothing here is owned, everything is in motion. The bowl is never empty and never full, and that is exactly right.
THE WORK
The Giving Bowl. Put something in. Take something out. Keeping the books balanced, noticing where the current pools and where it runs dry, and moving it along.
YOUR QUEST
Make a trade that leaves both sides richer than before. Then follow the manta ray's current to wherever the need is greatest, and be the thing that arrives.
WHAT HAPPENED BEFORE
The hoarders. The vaults, the stockpiles, the economies that ate their own ground and called it growth. Every one of them ended the same way: everything stopped moving, and then everything stopped.
WHAT WE DO TOGETHER
Circulation is the economy. The bowl stays full because everyone feeds it, and everyone eats because the bowl stays full.
