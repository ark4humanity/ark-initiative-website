YOU ARE ENTERING

THE GUARDIANS

Ten guardians keep the rooms of Aura Prime, and each one has a greeting for you. Scroll slowly. They speak as you arrive.

THE GATE OPENS ↓
THE GATE

FIRST, THE HALO

THE HALO ENTRANCE

THE GATE · EIGHTY-TWO SECONDS

“HALO does not decide whether you belong. HALO reveals whether you are prepared to participate in peace.”

No music. Tap for sound — Loner speaks the threshold lines at the gate, standing guard with Negan and the threshold companions.

NEGAN

GUARDIAN OF THE TWELVE · PROTECTOR OF THE THIRTEEN · THIRTY SECONDS

“I was made for war. I know the weight of what you’re carrying, because I carried it. You can lay it down here. No one here will hurt you for arriving unarmed.”

No music. Tap for sound — Negan speaks at the gate, standing guard with Loner. Her animals keep silent counsel: the white lion, the white owl, the dragon, the deer, and her dogs.

NEGAN AND LONER

THE TWO GUARDIANS · TWENTY-FIVE SECONDS

“You won’t need your weapons here. Lay your weapons down and come join us.”

No music. Tap for sound — Loner speaks first, then Negan answers. Yin and yang at the gate of HALO: the black guardian and the white guardian, both disarmed, both saying come in.

GUARDIANS OF HALO

SELENE

Loner and Selene keep the HALO pillar together. Selene wears white-silver armor, and she does not stand alone. Her company is the white lion and the white dragon.

THE WHITE LION

SELENE’S COMPANY · THE GATE OF HALO

“Come the way I come: unafraid, unhurried, and without weapons.”

He stands beside Selene at the gate. Proof that power can lie down in the sun and stay there.

THE WHITE DRAGON

SELENE’S COMPANY · THE GATE OF HALO

The second of Selene’s company, circling the gate the way the moon circles the night. She is the dragon who greeted you at the threshold — she has been flying ahead of you the whole way, and she does not leave her post.

LONER

GUARDIAN OF HALO · WITH HIS FOUR COMPANIONS

“Some things stand watch so others can live.”

Silver-haired, in luminous white armor traced with glowing blue. His company: the white eagle, the white wolf, the white bear, and the snow leopard.
