HALO · THE THIRD ROOM

THE JOINING

Not alone. Not anymore.

“Long have I kept this threshold.”
“Not alone. Not anymore.”
“Then I lay down my solitude with my sword.”
“And I lay down my war beside it. Take my hand, Loner.”

This is the room past the laying down and past the silence. Loner kept the threshold alone for a long time. Negan was made for war. Both of them set it down, and then they did the harder thing: they reached for each other.

“Our fates are intertwined now, Loner, then let us face whatever comes together.”

Here is where you stop arriving and start belonging. Take a hand. It does not matter whose. The grip is the same, and it says two things: I will not hurt you, and I will not leave you.

Beyond this room the twelve paths open, one for each pillar. You do not have to choose yet. You only have to be willing to walk beside someone while you decide.
