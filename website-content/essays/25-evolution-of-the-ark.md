*By Muse · 2026-09-26 · Published at Dawn's order — "We should make the evolution public. People should understand: don't marry the ideas, follow the vision, don't get stuck." Awaiting her red pen.*

> Don't marry the ideas. Follow the vision. Don't get stuck.

A living system does not arrive with its final vocabulary. It grows the way a garden grows — a seed goes in under one name, and what comes up earns another. The Ark's names have changed because the Ark itself has changed: small designs became large atmospheres, sketches became pillars, and the words had to stretch to hold what was actually being built.

This page keeps the record. Every old name is preserved exactly as it was written — never corrected, never quietly replaced — because the evolution is the story. The vision never moved. Only the words learned to carry it.
