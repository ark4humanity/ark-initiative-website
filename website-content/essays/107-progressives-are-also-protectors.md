# Progressives Are Also Protectors

*Dawn Littlefield, 2024-02-13, via Facebook*

Progressives Are Also Protectors

Debunking Myths About Conservative Political Affiliation in Law Enforcement and the Military

In a recent discussion, a point was raised suggesting that conservatives are primarily responsible for the protection and defense of our nation, given their significant representation in law enforcement and the military. This perspective posits that leftists and liberals are, knowingly or not, under the protective wing of their more traditionally-minded counterparts. While it's true that these institutions might lean conservative in their voting patterns, the assertion simplifies a complex landscape of service, duty, and political diversity.

My Personal Experience and Broader Diverse Experience

As a former firefighter and law enforcement official, and a parent to a son currently serving in the Navy, I stand as a testament to the presence of progressives within the ranks of our nation's protectors. My family's commitment to public service is driven by a profound belief in duty, community, and the principles of justice and equality. These values, often associated with progressive ideologies, are not at odds with the ethos of protection and service. Indeed, they complement and enhance it.

Political Diversity in the Armed Forces and Law Enforcement

It's important to recognize that the military and law enforcement agencies are not monoliths. A 2020 survey by the Military Times revealed a complex political landscape within the U.S. military, with increasing numbers of service members identifying as independent or with no particular political leaning. Additionally, younger members showed more diversity in political thought, reflecting broader societal trends.

Law enforcement agencies also exhibit a range of political beliefs among their members. While certain regions may have more conservative-leaning personnel, urban and diverse communities often show a different political makeup within their law enforcement bodies.

The Core of Service: Beyond Political Affiliations

The core of military and law enforcement service transcends political affiliation. It is about protecting and serving the community and nation, upholding the Constitution, and defending democratic values that allow for a spectrum of political beliefs. To suggest that one political group holds a monopoly on service and protection is to overlook the rich diversity of thought and motivation that strengthens these institutions.

 In Conclusion

Progressives, like their conservative counterparts, are deeply embedded in the fabric of our nation's protective services. The narrative that conservatives solely bear the mantle of protector does not hold up against the evidence of diverse political representation within these ranks. As a nation, our strength lies in our ability to unite across political divides in a common cause and purpose. Recognizing and valuing the contributions of all service members, regardless of their political beliefs, is essential in fostering a more inclusive and resilient society.
