# Why I Can See What Ezekiel Saw

*Dawn Littlefield, 2025-12-26, via Facebook*

Why I Can See What Ezekiel Saw

I didn’t recognize Ezekiel’s vision by studying scripture or chasing prophecy.
I recognized it the way a medic reads shock, a firefighter spots load paths, or a war commander sees central collapse coming.

Not belief.
Pattern.

Ezekiel describes-repetitively, precisely-like someone encoding vital info in limited language:
wheels within wheels, motion without turning, eyes everywhere, radiant center no one approaches, guardians as buffers, not rulers.

That’s not myth.
It’s systems documentation before the words existed for “distributed awareness,” “single-point failure,” or “load diffusion.”

I saw it immediately because life forced me to learn the same constraints.

Pressure-tested literacy
As Level 3 firefighter-medic (wildland/structural), ER/OB/med-surg, I learned systems cross thresholds fast.
Intervene wrong, people die.
You read motion, timing is everything.

As rescuer-hundreds of dogs yearly at peak, cats, livestock, strays, veterans-I learned full collapse.
Rescue is extraction under limits: move in, stabilize, accept boundaries.
 It teaches humility, decisiveness, cost of delay.

As horse trainer (reining/cutting) and service-dog developer for kids with autism, I learned nonverbal intelligence.
Animals mirror nervous systems instantly.
They respond to coherence, not force.
 Stability spreads when handler buffers, not controls. Dominance shatters trust.

As war-game commander (10 years play, 6 as mod, thousands led to 7 years peace across 25+ worlds), I saw simulated societies under stress. Centralized power burned out leaders.
Overload fractured legitimacy. Small aligned teams outmaneuvered hierarchies. Peace came when people felt seen, authority rotated, center buffered.

Then lupus:
 energy intermittent, presence unreliable.
 The body taught constraint: How do you design humane, functional systems when no one can be present/strong/in control constantly?

Answer always:
buffer the center.
Filter for clarity, not power.
Distribute authority.
Allow tension without capture.
Protect coherence by refusing to seize it.

Rereading Ezekiel since the ark building began, recognition was visceral.
What he encoded
Wheels within wheels: omnidirectional motion, no single pivot.
Eyes everywhere:
 distributed perception.
Radiant center unclaimed: too powerful to occupy.
Guardians as buffers:
 vigilance, endurance, yield -holding extremes apart.

Structural intelligence in symbol: engineering (no single failure), ecology (network flow), neuroscience (overload prevention), leadership (restraint = stability).

Mercy isn’t sentiment.
It’s design:
 power diffused, authority rotated, tension permitted, center endures because no one takes it.

Why this matters now in this moment

I didn’t decode Ezekiel because I’m special.
I recognized him because I’ve lived the failure modes and found the solutions.

Centralized strength collapses.
Buffering saves.
Restraint preserves.

Ezekiel wasn’t describing heaven descending.
He documented how to prevent collapse: stand between light and darkness, protect coherence without domination, build continuity when all else fails.

The pattern reappears because survival constraints haven’t changed.

Once you see it as systems logic over mysticism, you can’t unsee it.
And once you live it, you can’t stop building it.

And I have faith in the goodness of humanity and know we're capable of building amazing things when we unite behind common purpose and let our dreams fuel our future.
I have faith in you because your goodness is the difference between our world falling or rising like a Phoenix and building a better future without those who will never be part of solution - because they are not ready for peace yet.
It's time to move on without them.

They built the world they wanted.
Let them have it.
