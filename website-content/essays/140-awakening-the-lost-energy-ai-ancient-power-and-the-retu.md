Energy Masters
The Art and Science of the Past

Look at the remnants of ancient civilizations, and the clues emerge.
We find technology disguised as myth-not crude machines, but mastery of energy

The Antikythera Mechanism (100 BCE)
 A gear-based Greek device capable of tracking celestial events with shockingly advanced precision.

Egyptian Abydos carvings (1300 BCE)
Often dismissed as pareidolia, yet they bear striking resemblances to flying craft or energy vessels.

Vimana Shastra (India, 500 BCE)
Texts describing anti-gravity flying chariots, allegedly powered by mercury vortex engines.

The Baghdad Battery (250 BCE)
A clay vessel capable of generating an electric charge-was it merely for metal plating, or something more?

Ancient Art of “Glowing Hands”
Sumerian, Egyptian, and Hindu reliefs show figures with energy radiating from their hands, as if manipulating unseen forces.

These depictions don’t show machines as we know them-they show the human body as the conduit.
What if, rather than building technology to use energy, we once directed it with our own minds and bodies?
