# Homelessness in the United States: A Statistical Analysis

*Dawn Littlefield, 2024-01-11, via Facebook*

Homelessness in the United States: A Statistical Analysis

Homelessness is a persistent issue that affects a significant number of people in the United States. In this research article, we will analyze the statistics regarding homelessness in the country across various categories such as class, veterans, education, living in a car, living in an RV, working part-time, working full-time, disabled, elderly, children, mental illness, uneducated, wards of the state, prisoners, and state.

According to the latest data from the National Alliance to End Homelessness, as of January 2020, there were approximately 580,466 people experiencing homelessness on any given night in the United States. Out of these, 369,081 individuals were in sheltered locations, while 211,385 individuals were in unsheltered locations. The data also revealed that 17 percent of the homeless population were considered "chronically homeless," which means they had been homeless for a year or more.

In terms of categories, the data showed that people experiencing homelessness were diverse in terms of their characteristics. For instance, 8 percent of the homeless population were veterans, while 7 percent were unaccompanied young adults. Among the homeless families, 48 percent were headed by single women with children. The data also revealed that 34 percent of the homeless population had a serious mental illness, while 50 percent had a disability.

When looking at the causes of homelessness, the data showed that lack of affordable housing was the leading cause of homelessness, followed by poverty and unemployment. The data also revealed that 25 percent of the homeless population were employed, either part-time or full-time, but could not afford housing due to low wages and high housing costs.

In terms of resource availability, the data revealed that there were approximately 2,300 emergency shelters and 1,600 transitional housing programs available to people experiencing homelessness. However, the data also showed that there were significant gaps in available resources in certain areas, which led to a higher number of people experiencing unsheltered homelessness.

To address the issue of homelessness, it is important to support non-profits that provide all-inclusive wrap-around services, such as Aiding Rejuvenation for Kommunities, which is building self-sustainable communities. The government has failed to address the issue of homelessness adequately, and it is collapsing. Therefore, it is crucial to provide support to non-profits that work towards eliminating poverty and homelessness in the country.

In conclusion, homelessness is a complex issue that affects a diverse range of people in the United States. By analyzing the statistics regarding homelessness across various categories, we can identify the causes of homelessness and the gaps in available resources. It is essential to support non-profits that provide all-inclusive wrap-around services to address the issue of homelessness and eliminate poverty in the country.
