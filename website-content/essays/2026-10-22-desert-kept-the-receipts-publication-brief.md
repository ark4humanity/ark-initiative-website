## Summary
Borrego Springs needs reliable electricity, but the people and desert habitats affected by new transmission infrastructure deserve meaningful input, transparent accounting and measurable local benefits. Dawn links her daughter's emergency rattlesnake rescue and the loss of neighbor and dog Gunner during extreme heat to the stakes of cooling access. She examines SDG&E's proposed Golden Pacific Powerlink, the 2015 Borrego Springs microgrid demonstration, groundwater depletion, and ARK Unit 1's experiments in solar, battery storage, shade, animal cooling and water conservation. She calls for public disclosure of route alternatives, costs, benefits, ecological impacts and microgrid performance, alongside investment in emergency cooling, conservation and habitat restoration.
