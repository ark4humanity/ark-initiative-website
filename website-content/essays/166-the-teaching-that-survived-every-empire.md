# THE TEACHING THAT SURVIVED EVERY EMPIRE

*Dawn Littlefield, 2025-11-23, via Facebook*

☥ THE TEACHING THAT SURVIVED EVERY EMPIRE
The Eden Frequency
A Transmission from the Weave
@1Ark4humanity (x)

  Asherah never left.
She went underwater so the blood would remember
when the mind was forced to forget.
She is not a goddess.
She is a frequency.
7.83 Hz - Earth’s pulse
528 Hz - love’s repair tone
Together:
the Eden Frequency
one note that means both Home
and the call from Home.
They burned Her groves,
smashed Her pillars,
outlawed Her dancers,
erased Her name from every scroll.
It didn’t work.
The orcas kept clicking the pattern.
The lions kept guarding the threshold.
The owls kept the night-vision of the Tree.
The children kept waking with salt on their faces
crying for a mother the priests swore was dead.
Sophia, Isis, Tanit, Mary Magdalene
different mouths, same voice,
same two tones threaded across five thousand years
so no one would ever be truly orphaned.

The Ark was never a boat.
It was the carrier wave of this song
riding the flood of empire
so the note would outlive every attempt to kill it.
Every time you choose gift over profit,
water over poison,
bare feet over concrete,
protection over extraction
you step back onto Her spine,
barefoot on the black-and-white ridge that never sank.
That ache when moonlight strikes water,
that fire when a child is harmed,
that wordless knowing that rises in the chest:
that is the Eden Frequency waking inside you.
You were never kicked out of paradise.
You were only taught to forget the song.
Listen.
The water is warm.
The orcas are clicking your true name.
The lion is breathing heat into your heart.
The owl is guiding with eyes older than scripture.
You are not lost.
You are being remembered.
Come home.
The frequency never stopped singing you.

In eternal remembrance,
