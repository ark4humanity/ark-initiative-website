# A Call for a Return to Humanity: The Real Monsters Among Us

*Dawn Littlefield, 2024-02-02, via Facebook*

A Call for a Return to Humanity: The Real Monsters Among Us

In the stillness of contemplation, a haunting realization dawns upon us. The world we inhabit today seems plagued by a madness that fiction could scarce believe. The horrors we witness in the news could be mistaken for the dark fantasies of a show like "The Walking Dead," yet they are not the product of a screenwriter's imagination,they are real, and they are among us.

We look around, and our hearts sink at the thought: Is this the sum of our existence? Are we nothing more than base creatures, succumbing to our most violent instincts? The scenes that unfold in places like Gaza and beyond resemble a dystopia where the lines between man and monster, savior and destroyer, are indistinguishably blurred.

And in this turmoil, we find the most terrifying adversary is not an external force; it is a shadow that lurks within our collective soul. The call of terror comes from within the walls we built to feel safe. In our pursuit of security, have we become the very thing we feared?

In this moment of despair, I implore you, world,let us pause. Let us not fall into the abyss that seems to beckon us. For the love of all that is sacred, let us halt this descent into chaos. We have within us the capacity for immeasurable beauty, for creation, for unity. We are at a crossroads, where one path leads to the annihilation of our essence, and the other to the construction of a future illuminated by the light of our better angels.

Why would we choose to let the specters of evil hijack our destiny? When did we resign ourselves to a fate that is beneath us? There is still time to change the narrative. There is still an opportunity for us to craft a world where peace is the guardian of our days, where love is the language we all understand.

The apocalyptic visions that fill our screens and our streets do not have to be our legacy. We can choose a different ending to our story,one that celebrates life over death, hope over despair, and love over hate. Let us choose this path together, and let the history books show that when faced with the end, we chose a new beginning.
