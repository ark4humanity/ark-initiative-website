# Thou Shall Not Pass

*Dawn Littlefield, 2024-03-13, via Facebook*

Thou Shall Not Pass

Standing in the Breach for Separation of Church and State

The image from history, as stark as it is, reminds us of the dark chapters when the powers of church and state were not just entwined but disastrously fused. It depicts a machine reportedly used to break the hands and fingers of those deemed heretics, symbolizing an era when dissenting voices,be they scientists or artists,were met not with debate, but with ruthless oppression.

This bleak relic serves as a sobering reminder of the importance of maintaining the separation of church and state, a principle enshrined in the U.S. Constitution. The founding fathers, well aware of the tumultuous history of religious persecution in Europe, sought to create a society where religious freedom is protected and the government remains neutral in matters of faith.

We must be vigilant to ensure that history does not repeat itself. The walls that safeguard the separation of church and state must be fortified by constant awareness and proactive defense. In the breach stands not just lawmakers or judges, but every citizen, tasked with the preservation of this fundamental principle.

The lessons from history are clear: when religious authority becomes entangled with governmental power, the path to tyranny is short. To honor the progress made, we must reject any regression into the shadows of the past. The principle of 'Thou shall not pass' must apply to any encroachment upon the separation that keeps the fabric of a diverse and free society intact.

Let us heed the warning of history and continue to stand firm in the breach, ensuring that the realms of religious belief and civil governance do not cross the boundaries that have kept our society free and our people diverse in their faiths yet united in their pursuit of liberty.
