# You Think It'll Fail? Challenge Accepted

*Dawn Littlefield, 2024-03-03, via Facebook*

You Think It'll Fail? Challenge Accepted

Restoring Faith in Humanity
 Building ARks for a Star Trek Future

"Give me 10 years and enough funding," I say to the skeptics like Duke Ward, and watch as ARks 4 Humanity transforms skepticism into belief. This is not a plea,it's a challenge, a dare to those doubting Thomases who see the glass as half empty. Invest in this project, and prepare to witness the unveiling of a society where mutual support and shared progress are not just ideals but tangible realities.

Doubters question the incentive to work when basic needs are universally met. "Why work?" they ask. They fail to grasp the intrinsic nature of human beings to seek purpose beyond mere survival. Universal Basic Income (UBI) is not about discouraging work; it's about valuing human life and potential over the economic rat race. It's a trampoline for innovation, not a hammock for laziness.

At ARks 4 Humanity, we are not just dreamers; we are architects of a future where work is an expression of self, not a yoke of necessity. In our envisioned communities, a janitor or a nurse stands as tall as any CEO, for their work is dignified not by its hardships but by its necessity and nobility.

We stand at a crossroads, facing a choice between surrendering to despair or marching towards a new dawn. We choose the latter. We choose to forge a future where our advancements in technology and society light the way,a Star Trek future that is within our grasp.

Join us, not just in hope, but in action. Join ARks 4 Humanity on this audacious quest to reconstruct the soul of society. Invest in us, challenge us, and in 10 years, we'll return with a new chapter of human history,one where faith in humanity is not only restored but celebrated.

Dawn Littlefield, Founder of ARks 4 Humanity
